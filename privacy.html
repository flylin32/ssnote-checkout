Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Summary

ssNote is designed to keep your notes on your own device. We do not have a server that stores your notes, and we do not collect, read, sell or share your note content.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your notes, tags, outlines, or any text you write in the App. We do not require an account, and we do not run analytics or advertising SDKs that profile your content.

3. Information processed when you buy the Pro upgrade

Payments are handled by Paddle.com Market Limited ("Paddle"), acting as Merchant of Record. When you purchase, Paddle processes the information needed to complete the payment, such as your email address, country, payment method details and tax information. Paddle acts as an independent controller for that data and handles it under its own privacy notice. We receive only limited information, such as confirmation that a purchase was completed and the transaction identifier, which we use solely to unlock Pro features and to handle support and refund requests.

Paddle's privacy notice: paddle.com/legal/privacy

4. Data stored on your device

The App stores your settings and purchase status locally on your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes this local information.

5. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not set our own cookies or track visitors. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and user agent) for security and operational purposes. If you follow a link to Paddle's checkout, Paddle's own cookie and privacy practices apply.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold an account or a copy of your notes, most data-subject requests can be satisfied on your device by editing or deleting your own data. For personal data processed by Paddle in connection with a purchase, please contact Paddle. You may also contact us and we will help where we can.

8. Security

Payments are transmitted over encrypted connections and processed by Paddle's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure. We never see or store your full card details.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page with its "Last updated" date.
